Higgins Gives Answers. HAL Takes Action.

  
Two AI layers sit behind the dashboard, and they do different jobs on purpose.


  

    

      
Ask Higgins

      
Gives Answers

      
An omnipresent, context-aware assistant. It reads your role, active tab, current tasks and calendar to answer accurately — it never changes anything on its own.

      
    
        
  • Answers what's due, what's blocked, what's coming up
    • 
        
  • Explains the S.A.L.E., P.I.T.C.H., and P.U.R.E.S. frameworks
    • 
        
  • Surfaces and retrieves the right report or resource
    • 
        
  • Escalates to a human specialist when a question needs one
    • 
      

    

    

      
HAL — Higgins Automations Lab

      
Takes Action

      
The execution layer. HAL extends Ask Higgins with the ability to actually do the thing, inside a defined, auditable boundary.

      
    
        
  • Creates, updates, and completes tasks on your behalf
    • 
        
  • Schedules and moves calendar blocks around commitments
    • 
        
  • Drafts email responses for review — never sends without you
    • 
        
  • Updates weekly goals as priorities shift, with a visible activity log
    • 
      

    

  


  

    
The boundary, in one line

    
If it's a question, Ask Higgins answers it. If it's a task — create it, move it, draft it, schedule it — HAL executes it and logs what it did, so the system stays fast without ever feeling like a black box.

  







  
The Three Scoring Engines

  
Every Higgins engagement runs on the same logic — score first, then act — applied to three different questions.


  

    

      
🎯 P.U.R.E.S.

      
Discover — scores prospects worth pursuing.

      

        
P
Profitability — is there enough margin/value here?

        
U
Urgency — is there a pressing, near-term need?

        
R
Reachability — can you get in front of a decision-maker?

        
E
Engagement — has the prospect shown any signal of interest?

        
S
Sustainability — is this durable, not a one-off?

      

    

    

      
🚀 S.A.L.E.

      
Accelerate — the full client-acquisition loop.

      

        
S
Sourcing — build the qualified pipeline (uses P.U.R.E.S.).

        
A
Assessing — classify buying pattern & decision timeline.

        
L
Listening — collect feedback, route it to the next action.

        
E
Empowering — protect retention, drive referrals & growth.

      

    

    

      
💰 P.I.T.C.H.

      
Fund — scores investor & capital-source fit.

      

        
P
Positioning — does their thesis match your sector?

        
I
Investment Capacity — can they write a meaningful check?

        
T
Traction/Stage Fit — does your stage match their mandate?

        
C
Contactability — is there a real path to a named contact?

        
H
Heft/Seniority — is the contact senior enough to say yes?

Terminology Guide

  
Plain-English definitions for every term used across Higgins Academy.


  

  

    
P.U.R.E.S.
Profitability, Urgency, Reachability, Engagement, Sustainability — the five-dimension scoring engine that sorts prospects into Tier 1A/1B/1C.

    
S.A.L.E.
Sourcing, Assessing, Listening, Empowering — the full client-acquisition loop, from a scored prospect list to referral-driven growth.

    
P.I.T.C.H.
Positioning, Investment Capacity, Traction/Stage Fit, Contactability, Heft/Seniority — a 100-point fit score for ranking investors and capital sources.

    
Ask Higgins
The omnipresent, context-aware chatbot layer. Answers questions about your workspace and the frameworks — never changes anything on its own.

    
HAL (Higgins Automations Lab)
The action-taking AI layer. Creates and completes tasks, moves calendar blocks, and drafts (never auto-sends) email — every action lands in a visible activity log.

    
Four-Quadrant Dashboard
The home screen: To Do, Calendar, Reports & Documents, and Top of Mind — one AI-assisted view instead of tab-hopping.

    
Tier 1A / 1B / 1C
The three P.U.R.E.S. outreach tiers — 1A is the highest-scoring, highest-priority group of prospects.

    
Move the Needle
A specific, high-impact, non-routine action completed daily to advance the week's stated intention.

    
Weekly Intention
One primary goal set for the week, with a measurable success metric and daily progress tracking.

    
Group Hub
A shared workspace for a team — pipelines and target lists shared with edit/view roles and full change attribution.

    
Report Stage
Every Higgins report moves through Stage 1 (Directional), Stage 2 (Draft), and Stage 3 (Final) — each with a specific review job.

    
Stage 1 — Directional
The first version of a Higgins report or curriculum. Confirms overall direction and approach before deeper work begins.

    
Stage 2 — Draft
A full working draft with most content in place. The client reviews and suggests improvements.

    
Stage 3 — Final
The near-complete, polished version. Only fine-tuning is needed before sign-off.

    
Fit Score
The 0–100 total from a P.I.T.C.H. scoring pass, sorting investors into four outreach tiers (Priority down to Emerging).

    
Activity Log
The auditable record of every action HAL has taken on a user's behalf, separate from the general chat history.
