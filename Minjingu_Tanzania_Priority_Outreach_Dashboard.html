What to do with it

Work it top down — the list is sorted by P.U.R.E.S. score, so the vendor most worth your time is first. Open any card with View / Contact and you get a ready-to-send email written for that vendor's type, a text message, and a phone script that already names their region and soil condition.

Every message ends with the Fertilizer Selection & Order link, so a recipient who is interested can match the right Minjingu product to their soils and place an order without waiting for a follow-up call.

Use the My status dropdown on each card to track where each conversation stands. It saves in your browser, so the list remembers what you did last time you opened it.

The single strongest argument, for almost every vendor here: imported DAP locks up in acidic soil within 2–3 weeks, so the farmer pays for phosphorus the crop never gets. Minjingu's rock phosphate dissolves slowly, driven by the soil's own acidity, and keeps feeding for months. The dealer earns a better margin and the farmer comes back next season.

What is deliberately not here

The other 149 vendors in the full network are not rejects. Most are real TFRA-licensed dealers with no findable phone or email — they need a call through TFRA or a regional cooperative office, or a field visit, and they belong in a different kind of campaign. The rest are Tier 3–4, competitors, or flagged records. They all live in the full Tanzania Vendor Network dashboard.

How the Scoring Works

The standard Higgins P.U.R.E.S. system scores a prospect 0–20 on five dimensions for a total out of 100. Because these are distribution partners rather than end buyers, each dimension is measured differently than in the estate-and-cooperative reports.

LetterWhat it measures hereWhat drives the score
P — ProfitabilityHow much volume this vendor could realistically moveTFRA licence class is the best available proxy: Importer 19 · Wholesaler 17 · Distributor 16 · Dealer 13 · Retailer 11. Cooperative unions floor at 16 because they buy in bulk for members. Competitors cap at 13.
U — UrgencyHow badly this vendor's customers need the product nowRegional fertilizer-demand intensity: core acidic-soil region 17 · partly acidic 14 · Dar es Salaam import hub 13 · other 11. Importers and wholesalers gain +2 for seasonal restock cycles.
R — ReachHow many farms one relationship touchesCooperative unions and AMCOS floor at 18 — they aggregate whole districts. Importers 18, wholesalers 16, retailers 9. Dar es Salaam gains +2 for national distribution from the port.
E — EaseHow quickly you could actually start a conversationContact channels on file (6 + 3 per channel), adjusted for distance from Arusha and whether the source was verified. Records flagged "qualify first" lose 3.
S — SynergyHow well Minjingu's specific product fitsAcidic-soil region +5, partly acidic +3, cooperative/organic story +2, bio-fertilizer co-formulation +4, home-market logistics +2. Competitors −6, split-focus agrovet/vet shops −3.
