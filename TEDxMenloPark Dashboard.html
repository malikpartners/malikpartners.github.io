Eight AI applications, one stage

Each seat owns an application of AI to a global challenge. Heard together, they argue for deliberately directing AI at these problems.

01 · Health

AI & Global Health

AI‑assisted diagnostics, triage, and care coordination that extend scarce clinical expertise into underserved regions.

02 · Poverty

AI & Economic Inclusion

AI‑driven financial inclusion, credit access, and livelihood tools for populations excluded from traditional systems.

03 · Education

AI & Equitable Education

Adaptive learning and tutoring systems that extend individualized instruction to students who'd otherwise never get it.

04 · Homelessness

AI & Coordinated Care

AI‑assisted case coordination connecting the fragmented housing, health, and social‑service systems behind homelessness.

05 · Human Rights

AI & Human Rights

Algorithmic bias, surveillance risk, and the digital‑rights safeguards that must travel with AI deployed at social scale.

06 · Environment

AI & Climate

Climate modeling, resource optimization, and conservation applications where AI is already changing outcomes.

07 · Policy

AI Governance & Ethics

What responsible, coordinated AI deployment for public‑good problems actually requires from policy and industry.

08 · Infrastructure

AI & the Digital Divide

Compute access and connectivity — and the risk that AI's benefits concentrate where its capital already does.
