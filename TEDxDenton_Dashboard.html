Eight disciplines, one stage

Each seat owns part of the solution. Heard together, they argue for coordination over any single fix.

01 · Housing & Shelter

Housing & Shelter Systems

Permanent supportive housing and Housing First economics in a mid‑sized Texas metro.

02 · Health

Behavioral & Public Health

Co‑occurring mental illness and substance use among the chronically unhoused — and what coordinated care requires.

03 · Government

Municipal Government & Policy

What city government can and can't do alone — zoning, encampment policy, the limits of regulation.

04 · Public Safety

Law Enforcement

Reframing policing from enforcement‑first to a coordinated‑referral partner.

05 · Workforce

Workforce & Economic Mobility

Employment pathways and what "self‑sufficiency" realistically requires after housing.

06 · Education

Education & Youth (McKinney‑Vento)

Homelessness as it appears inside Denton ISD classrooms — and what schools already know.

07 · Nonprofit

Faith & Community Nonprofits

The largest existing delivery network for emergency shelter and food — and where it's stretched thin.

08 · Technology

Applied Technology / AI Coordination

Data‑sharing and AI‑assisted case coordination that connects fragmented systems without replacing judgment.
